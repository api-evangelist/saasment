# Saasment - AI-Powered SaaS & Cloud Cost Optimization

## About Saasment

Saasment is an AI-powered platform that automatically finds and eliminates waste in your SaaS, cloud, and AI spending. Companies save 20-40% on software costs with zero effort.

## What We Do

- **AI Cost Detection**: Our AI agents continuously scan your software stack to identify unused licenses, redundant tools, and optimization opportunities.
- **Automated Savings**: We don't just find waste—we fix it. Automated actions handle license rightsizing, resource optimization, and renewal management.
- **Cloud Optimization**: Reduce AWS, GCP, and Azure spend through reserved instance optimization, idle resource detection, and right-sizing recommendations.
- **SaaS Management**: Get visibility into shadow IT, consolidate duplicate tools, and optimize license utilization across your organization.

## Key Features

1. **AI Agents**: Autonomous agents that monitor spending 24/7 and take action automatically
2. **100+ Integrations**: Connect to all your SaaS, cloud, and financial tools
3. **Real-time Dashboard**: Live visibility into spend, savings, and optimization opportunities
4. **Managed Services**: Optional white-glove support for vendor negotiations and complex optimizations
5. **Enterprise Security**: SOC 2 Type II certified, SSO/SAML, read-only integrations
